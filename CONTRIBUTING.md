# Contributing to Blockparty

Thanks for helping build Blockparty. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the
relevant authority in [docs/README.md](docs/README.md) before changing code,
content, or documentation.

## License and provenance

- Code, tests, configuration, and build tooling are contributed under
  `AGPL-3.0-or-later`.
- Original game content and assets are contributed under `CC BY-SA 4.0`.
- The project does not currently accept a contribution as an assignment of
  copyright. Contributors must have the right to grant the stated license and
  must identify any separate owner or restrictions before submission.
- Every creative or numerical asset needs a provenance record covering creator,
  date, inputs/sources, license, attribution, AI-tool record where applicable,
  and review. Do not submit copied, paraphrased, extracted, scanned, traced, or
  source-derived game material.
- Third-party code, fonts, icons, audio, images, and models must retain their
  upstream license and required notice. Add the item to [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)
  and do not assume that a downloadable asset permits web embedding.

## Change expectations

Use the active [classic overhaul queue](docs/delivery/classic-overhaul-backlog.md)
for scoped work, preserve the server-authority and deterministic-engine
invariants, and update [traceability](docs/traceability.md) with evidence.
Run the checks required by the ticket before requesting review. Never include
capabilities, private game data, or secrets in issues, logs, screenshots, test
fixtures, or analytics.
