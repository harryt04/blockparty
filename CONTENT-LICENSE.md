license (CC BY-SA 4.0), unless a file or its accompanying provenance record

<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>.

## Covered material

The CC BY-SA grant covers original creative and editorial material, including:

- the magical-city names, places, characters, card titles, card copy, tutorials,
  help text, translations, and other game prose;
- original board/deck/economy content records and their presentation data in
  `packages/game-content/`;
- original illustrations, token designs, icons, sounds, fonts, and other
  assets added to the repository with a provenance record; and
- original product-facing content in the `docs/product/`, `docs/brand/`, and
  `docs/design/` authorities where the document identifies the material as
  original content.

Attribution must retain the Blockparty project name, the source path or asset
identifier when practical, the CC BY-SA 4.0 link, and an indication of changes.
Adapted material must preserve the same license and attribution requirements.

- Application code, package source, tests, configuration, build scripts, and

- The Blockparty name, logos, and other marks are not granted by CC BY-SA or
  AGPL; see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).
- Third-party dependencies and assets retain their own licenses and notices.
  They are not relicensed by this file; see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
- Facts, ideas, methods of play, and material supplied under a separate
  written license are not claimed by this content grant.
