# Classic mechanical-completeness matrix

This is the exhaustive classic-baseline coverage checklist for the MVP in [PRD](prd.md). It is an internal product and test tool, not a public compatibility or affiliation claim. The accepted rules scale, board composition, and economy are product requirements; names, prose, art, card copy, interface assets, and presentation remain independently authored in [Game content](game-content.md). Implement [Glossary](glossary.md), [Rules](rules.md), and choose optional behavior only through [Rule variants](rule-variants.md). The matrix describes target coverage; the existing scaffold is not evidence that any row is complete.

## Research and expression boundary

Primary sources can identify uncopyrightable ideas or methods, but they are not production source material. Publisher-specific research, if counsel approves it, must be access-controlled, version-pinned, dated, and separated from expressive authorship and production assets. Record what factual behavior was extracted, who independently authored the requirement, and the similarity-review result.
