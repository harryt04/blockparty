# Requirements traceability

**Status:** implementation baseline
**Evidence state:** `Planned` until implementation, evidence, and approvals are linked

This register maps each normative family to its owner, required evidence, and
delivery loop. Ranges include every ID. A ticket updates its rows in the same
commit; a scaffold alone is never evidence.

## Engineering, protocol, security, analytics, and legal

B2 implements the separate snapshot/capability creation seam for ENG-010 and
the hash-only, cookie-only issuance control for SEC-002 in
`apps/web/src/server/games/create-game.ts`; B3 adds transactional invitation
admission, normalized pseudonyms, and separate seat/reclaim cookie issuance in
`apps/web/src/server/games/join-game.ts`. Evidence is in
`apps/web/test/create-game.test.ts`, `apps/web/test/join-game.test.ts`, and
`apps/web/test/join-route.test.ts`; command and transport controls are
implemented for B4 in `apps/web/src/server/commands/handle-command.ts` and the
commands Route Handler, with evidence in `apps/web/test/command-path.test.ts`.

B4 additionally provides the implementation and evidence for PRD-NFR-004,
ENG-015, PROTO-001, PROTO-002, and the command-authority portion of SEC-002.

B5 provides the field-by-field authorized projection seam in
`apps/web/src/server/projections/authorize.ts` and the authenticated persisted
bootstrap route in `apps/web/src/app/api/games/[gameId]/bootstrap/route.ts`.
`apps/web/test/projection.test.ts` and
`apps/web/test/bootstrap-route.test.ts` prove legal-action inclusion,
per-seat authority, schema validity, and exclusion of server secrets, future
deck order, private card identities, and internal fields.

B6 adds authenticated, bounded SSE admission in
`apps/web/src/app/api/games/[gameId]/events/route.ts`, ephemeral presence and
monotonic per-seat delivery in `apps/web/src/server/sse/registry.ts`, and the
MongoDB change-stream projection fan-out in
`apps/web/src/server/sse/change-stream.ts`. Evidence is in
`apps/web/test/events-route.test.ts` and `apps/web/test/sse.test.ts`, including
credential-free URLs, connection cleanup, per-seat filtering, keep-alives, and
change-stream lifecycle.

B7 adds durable bounded recovery selection in
`apps/web/src/server/sync/recovery.ts`, authenticated `/sync` responses, and
position-aware recovery frames on `/events`. Evidence is in
`apps/web/test/sync-route.test.ts` and `apps/web/test/events-route.test.ts`,
covering contiguous ranges, bounds, gaps, duplicates, unavailable history,
snapshot fallback, and seat authorization.

C1 adds the browser synchronization coordinator in
`apps/web/src/client/sync/sync-client.ts`, named-frame validation in
`apps/web/src/client/sync/event-source.ts`, and the React binding in
`apps/web/src/client/sync/use-game-sync.ts`. `apps/web/test/sync-client.test.ts`
proves credentialed bootstrap, authoritative snapshot replacement, contiguous
range recovery, malformed-frame recovery, terminal authorization handling, and
jittered reconnect state transitions; browser journey coverage remains with
the later application-surface test harness.
